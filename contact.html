Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for international trips and 1-2 months for domestic travel.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation 30+ days before travel: Full refund. 15-29 days: 50% refund. Within 14 days: Non-refundable.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage.

Can I customize my package?

Absolutely! Contact our travel consultants to create a personalized itinerary for your needs.
